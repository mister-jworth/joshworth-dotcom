My friend, client and collaborator Obie Scott Wade wrote a wonderful book about a quirky girl who was raised by hawks on the top ledge of a New York apartment. He asked me to design the cover, layout the contents and prepare it for publication. If you'd like to learn more about the book, or buy a copy, I also set up <a href="http://www.oddry.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">this web page</a> which will tell you everything you need to know.</p>
