<h2>Martiandise</h2>
Martiandise is a line of apparel that, some believe, came to us from the citizens of Mars. Others believe it was a joint project between myself and <a href="http://www.obieco.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ObieCo</a> in which some of my whimsical characters and designs were printed on t-shirts, wallets, and other apparel. We created a limited run of each item and sold them in a number stores around Los Angeles and Long Beach.

If you're curious, you can take a look at the original <a href="/uploads/2013/01/martiandise_linesheet.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Martiandise line-sheet (PDF)</a>.
