<div class="fb-col fb-col-33"><p><h2>Kantar Marketplace</h2>
Kantar is a global leader in the market research and consumer data business. Kantar Marketplace is an end-to-end research platform that gives their clients access to an impressive suite of research solutions. Working closely with Kantar's on-demand services division in Los Angeles, along with several programming teams in India and the branding team in London, I developed the visual design system for the entire site, along with a comprehensive style guide for internal designers to reference when building future components.</p>
