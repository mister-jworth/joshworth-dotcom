<h2>Blabbr</h2>
Blabbr is a site that lets you give a score to things you like or dislike. It was developed before Twitter really took off, and was an interesting take on the concept of what we've come to call "liking."

I designed the header image, and gave a facelift to an existing design. The animation at the top of the page changes based on how many "plus points" people had given that day.

Though it's not heavily trafficked, the site is still up and running.
