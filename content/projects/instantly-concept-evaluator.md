<h2>Instantly Concept Evaluator</h2>
If you're manufacturing one of the 16,000 new consumer products that hit the store shelves every year, you might want to get a quick read on whether people will want to buy it before you waste a lot of time and money on it. 
Concept Evaluator, a new tool by Instantly, allows you to upload some preliminary artwork, write a few words about your product and run it past an online army of consumers standing by to tell you what they think. 
To make this process quick and simple, I designed a streamlined, flexible interface that gives a quick read of how a product will perform in the marketplace.
