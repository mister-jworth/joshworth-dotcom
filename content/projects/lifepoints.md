<h2>LifePoints</h2>
Lightspeed Research hired me to develop the branding strategy, logo, app icon and all the visual elements of the interface for their mobile app which allows users to earn rewards by participating in market research studies.

If you're a fan of Amazon gift cards and you love answering questions about your cereal-buying habits, then you should definitely <a href="http://lifepointsapp.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">download the LifePoints app</a> and take it with you on your next shopping trip.
