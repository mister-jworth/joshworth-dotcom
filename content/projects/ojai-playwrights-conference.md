<div class="fb-col fb-col-33"><p><h2>Ojai Playwrights Conference</h2>
The Ojai Playwrights Conference nurtures the art of playwriting by hosting a world-class festival of new plays by established and emerging writers. I helped set up a website design to communicate their vision and allow their staff to keep the public up to date on the details for the festival, info about playwrights and upcoming fundraising events.</p>
