<div class="fb-col fb-col-33"><p><h2>PBS</h2>
PBS is the <a href="http://www-tc.pbs.org/about/media/about/cms_page_media/774/public-trust-2014.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">most trusted institution</a> in America, (above the US court system and newspaper publishers!) So it was an honor to travel to their Washington DC headquarters to help them formulate a new digital strategy.<br />
Working with Karen Reed from <a href="http://www.ipk.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IPK</a>, we spent a week interviewing key stakeholders and team members and uncovered a complex ecosystem of varied departments, each of them with brilliant ideas for how to capitalize on opportunities in the digital realm.<br />
We spoke with broadcasters, fundraisers, marketers, brand strategists, tech teams and top-level executives then consolidated their input into a comprehensive presentation with recommendations for a new digital direction.</p></div>
