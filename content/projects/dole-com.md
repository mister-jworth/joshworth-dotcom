<h2>Dole.com</h2>
Here's something you probably didn't know: If you spend enough time contemplating the taxonomy of fruit and vegetables, asking questions like "is grape a sub-category of fruit or of berries?" you find yourself contemplating subtle nuances of language, and eventually, around 2:45pm, the entire linguistic basis of thought and perception begins to wither away like the skin of a rotting peach. Then it's time for coffee.

I learned all about this while freelancing at <a href="http://www.iedesign.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IE Design + Communication</a> in Redondo Beach. I  was assigned the task of re-structuring the consumer-facing site for Dole.com, which had been envisioned as a resource for nutrition information, interwoven with product information. It had been partially designed with a nice look and feel but not much in the way of site architecture. I came up with a site taxonomy and consistent navigation elements, streamlined the page contents into re-usable modules, and offered ideas for how to make the site more useful. Once the page templates were designed, I became the liaison between the design studio and the offsite tech team responsible for getting the site up and running. Once this process was safely underway and everyone had their work cut out for them, I got to enjoy one of the main perks of consulting: Leaving for the next job.</div>
