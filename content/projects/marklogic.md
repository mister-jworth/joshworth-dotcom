<div class="fb-col fb-col-33"><p><h2>MarkLogic</h2>
Have you ever tried to explain something to a database programmer? Not likely, since they speak in an arcane lexicon that seems to have evolved from the days of reel-to-reel data storage. MarkLogic is a company that is doing a great job of modernizing the art of data management using a different programming paradigm, but they needed some help explaining it to an audience of database programmers. To get their message across, I designed and coded a scrollable animation that could visually reinforce some of the concepts contained in one of their introductory ebooks. I don't know if scrolling through it will turn you into a database programmer, but feel free to visit the site if you're into fancy moving diagrams.</p>
