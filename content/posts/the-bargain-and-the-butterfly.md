<p>Here's my new postcard design for a play by my brilliant friends over at <a href="http://ghostroad.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Ghost Road Company</a>. This one deals with genius, madness, and the creation of a synthetic soul. The play is rich with compelling concepts and imagery, so there was plenty to work with. I decided to focus on the intense concentration experienced by the main character, and on the concept that genius is about condensing a vast number of influences into something new.</p>
