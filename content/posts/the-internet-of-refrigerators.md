<p>The "next big thing" that technology people are predicting is the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_of_Things" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Internet of Things</a> – household devices that, for some reason, will be connected to the Internet. The most frequently cited example of this is the "smart fridge" that can send you a text message when you're out of mustard. Since this is something I'll need to be prepared for as a designer, I've gone ahead and mocked-up the screen interface for my future smart-fridge.</p>
