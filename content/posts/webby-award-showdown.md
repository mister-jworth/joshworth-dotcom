<p>Do you love voting for your favorite stuff? Do you get a thrill bestowing honor and prestige upon things with the click of a button? Do you enjoy the rush of power you feel when you're allowed to help decide the final outcome of a hard-fought, cut-throat competition? If so, you need to check out the fierce battle that's going on over at the <a href="http://pv.webbyawards.com/2015/web/general-website/science" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Webby - People's Voice Awards for the coveted Websites: Science category prize</a>. That's where you'll find the unlikely nominee "<a href="http://1pixelmoon.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">If the Moon Were Only 1 Pixel</a>," a home-made scale map of the solar system, competing against the well-staffed, fully-budgeted, and technically-superior projects presented by the likes of the BBC, Wired, and NASA. Does this little website even stand a chance? Probably not. But only you can decide for sure!</p>
<p><strong><a href="http://pv.webbyawards.com/2015/web/general-website/science" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cast your vote here</a></strong> and don't forget to tell your friends.</p>
