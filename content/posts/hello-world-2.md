<h4 style="text-align: center"><em>A picture of a mountain stream</em></h4>
When you stop and think about it, when you look around at things, it's all pretty great. There's just so much stuff. You've got...
<ul><br />
 	<li>trash bags</li>
 	<li>dragonflies</li>
 	<li>aspirin</li>
 	<li>jeans</li>
 	<li>cholesterol</li>
 	<li>the word "wow"</li>
 	<li>a picture of a mountain stream</li>
 	<li>speaker-wire</li>
 	<li>lightning</li>
 	<li>bullet lists</li>
 	<li>rubber duckies</li>
</ul>
... well - I'm not going to list it all here, but there's a lot, as I'm sure you've noticed. It would be nice if someone somewhere was keeping a list of everything that exists. (Is that one of the reasons we made computers - so we could create a way to catalogue everything so we don't lose track of it?) But is it a finite list? That's something for you to think about. Can something ever fall off this list? I guess no one would ever know. Does an item on the list count as something new that also needs to be added to the list... like this:
<ul><br />
 	<li>the pancakes I made this morning</li>
 	<li>the list item: "The pancakes I made this morning"</li>
 	<li>the list item: "the list item: The pancakes I made this morning"</li>
</ul>
... you see what I mean. It gets pretty messy. If you were the list-maker, keeping track of the items on the list itself would really take up most of your time. I wouldn't want to be the one in charge of it.</p>
<p>Let me know if you're keeping a list like this. I've got a few things to add.</p>
