<p>In his piece The Time Tunne<a title="View &quot;The Time Tunnel&quot; by Sean Carpone" href="http://www.seancapone.com/media/TimeTunnel_450x300.h264.mov" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">l</a> video artist <a title="visit Sean Carpone's website" href="http://www.seancapone.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Sean Carpone</a> has created "a mashup of every instance of 'time-travel-wormhole' motifs found in science fiction films."</p>
<p>Besides being totally awesome and mesmerizing to behold, the idea of compiling visual clichés into a single experience is pretty compelling. As you get sucked into these vortices, occasionally recognizing the movies they were grabbed  from (Buck Rogers! The Black Hole!) - you really start to understand that, every image in our culture is much more than a unique signifier for itself. It is also a subliminal reference to every occurrence of similar images used to signify the same thing. The images are words in our visual language.</p>
<p>For the time-travel-wormholes, the exact shapes, glowing lights, colors and all that are really nothing more than a new font choice for the visual sentence: "Holy crap! It's a time warp!"</p>
