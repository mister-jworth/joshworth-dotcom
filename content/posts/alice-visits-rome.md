<p>This is a projection loop I made for Maureen's production of Susan Sontag's play <a href="http://www.so-rimlee.com/literature-supernova/2011/5/6/susan-sontags-note-on-her-play-alice-in-bed.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Alice in Bed</a> at Loyola Marymount University. The video was projected on a hanging sheet during a monologue at the end of the first scene in which bed-ridden <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_James" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Alice James</a> imagines what it would be like to visit Rome.</p>
