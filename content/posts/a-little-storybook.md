<p>Here's a little book I made as a Kickstarter reward for people who backed the <a href="http://www.trade-city.org/2013/08/06/a-first-look-at-the-soft-camp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Soft Camp</a> - an art installation for kids I put together during the summer of 2013. The book introduces the characters and the setting, and was used as inspiration for the other artists who contributed to the project. Of course, the real story took place on the <a href="http://www.trade-city.org/the-popwagon/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Popwagon</a>, the mobile art stage of <a href="http://www.trade-city.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Trade City Productions</a>.</p>
