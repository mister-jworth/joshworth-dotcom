<details class="toggle"><summary>I think I more-or-less understand the difference between a conscious human being and an NPC in “Call of Duty.”</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>Though this weapon makes me look tough, I&#x27;m actually such a wuss that I&#x27;m threatened by the very notion of any lifestyle that isn&#x27;t confined to the limited borders of my comfort zone.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>The thought of wielding this firearm makes me feel like an action hero, even though pretty much every movie about vigilante justice ends with the message that a real hero is one who is brave enough to end the cycle of violence.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>This assault weapon is not outlawed because then only outlaws would own guns, and I&#x27;m not an outlaw and getting a gun should be super easy for everyone, including outlaws, because the easier it is to get guns, the easier it is to kill people with guns (but not schoolkids) because the more guns we have, the safer we&#x27;ll be from gun violence which is why I shouldn&#x27;t even have to fill out this form, because I&#x27;m totally not a psychopath, I just want a gun because I want to prevent gun deaths by killing people using my gun, because more guns on the street means fewer gun deaths, obviously. Stupid lib-tards. </summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>I acknowledge that this purchase is a knee-jerk substitute for an actual understanding of a changing world which often conflicts with an upbringing that taught me that compassion and empathy are a sign of weakness, and if I have to be this way, why are other people allowed to have feelings?</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>I understand that what I really need, more than this firearm, is a good cry on the shoulder of someone who understands my pain, but that would leave me vulnerable to my inner demons of sexual inadequacy, so I&#x27;d rather just make this purchase.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>Just because my father and I bonded over guns when I was a kid, this purchase is in no way a substitute for the genuine support and affection he has always withheld under the guise of &#x27;toughening me up for the real world.&#x27;</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>Though this firearm is large, it is in no way a substitute for what Sally Whitman once called a &#x27;tiny widdle wee-wee&#x27; when she caught me peeing in the hedges during recess.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>When I wield this firearm, I actually become more dangerous to society than the forces which I perceive to be a threat to my livelihood, most of which are imaginary projections of my own insecurity.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>Blasting this thing at an inanimate target may feel therapeutic, but until I unleash its power on the innocent, the world will never understand my true power. But I wouldn’t do that. Promise.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>The fact that I&#x27;m even considering the purchase of an assault weapon is a sign of my mental instability, but when people see me holding it, they&#x27;ll be too scared of me to probe into the specifics of my childhood trauma.</summary></details>
<details class="toggle"><summary>I understand that the frightened child who threatens my sense of power is cowering within my blackened soul, and is not an actual child in a school room.</summary></details>
