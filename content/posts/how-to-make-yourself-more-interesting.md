<ol>
 	<li>Learn to play <a title="learn it here" href="http://www.canasta.net/canasta_rules.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">canasta</a>. Become really really good. Get your friends together for a friendly game. Annihilate them in minutes. They'll never play you again, but they'll all say to each other, "Wow - he's really good at canasta"</li>
 	<li>Only eat with chopsticks. Even soup.</li>
 	<li>Always wear a tuxedo. (Goes for girls too.)</li>
 	<li>Create a dossier for each of your friends. Use a manila folder with their picture affixed to the front with a paperclip. Type their full name on the folder tab. Fill the folder with facts, documents, pictures, and printouts of all their emails. Whenever you get together with a friend, place the dossier on the table in front of them and leaf through it from time to time.</li>
 	<li>Grow a mustache. (Girls too.)</li>
 	<li>Regularly read <a title="read Pravda" href="http://english.pravda.ru/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pravda</a> - the Russian newspaper. When a current event comes up in conversation, you can say "I was just reading about that in Pravda"</li>
 	<li>Frequently stop talking mid-sentence, raise one eyebrow and say the name of a <a title="memorize these..." href="http://fabpedigree.com/james/mathmen.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">mathematician</a>. Resume conversation,</li>
 	<li>Bandage two fingers together. if someone asks what happened, just shake your head and sigh. Change the subject.</li>
 	<li>Tattoo the word "Hi" on your forehead.</li>
 	<li>Use Q-tips and toothpicks simultaneously.</li>
</ol>
