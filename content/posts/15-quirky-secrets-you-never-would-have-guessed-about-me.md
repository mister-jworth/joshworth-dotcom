<div>You may think you know everything about me and my life, but here are a few secrets that might surprise you!</div>
<ol>
 	<li>These are the same jeans I wore yesterday.</li>
 	<li>The finger I type with is the same one I use for poking things.</li>
 	<li>When the TV is muted, I can still tell who's talking by looking at whose mouth is moving.</li>
 	<li>I can open a door using only one hand. Right or left, doesn't matter.</li>
 	<li>I sort my clothes based on clothing type. Shirts go with shirts. Socks go with socks. Pants go with pants, etc.</li>
 	<li>When I'm rolling dice, and I'm trying for a six, I'll say, "Come on, six!"</li>
 	<li>When choosing a chair, I will always opt to sit in the one that looks most comfortable, unless it's already occupied.</li>
 	<li>If I come to visit your house and you offer me something to drink, I will usually accept, especially if I'm thirsty.</li>
 	<li>I have a pet peeve about waiters spilling hot coffee on my lap.</li>
 	<li>I choose greeting cards based on the occasion.</li>
 	<li>I'm not a stickler for time. If it's 10:58, sometimes I'll just say, "It's almost 11."</li>
 	<li>I'm very curious about odors. If there's a curious odor in the air, I'll often ask, "What's that odor?"</li>
 	<li>When I have to stick things together, I've been known to use tape, glue, or other adhesives.</li>
 	<li>I can kick your ass at badminton, unless you're a better player, or you get lucky or something.</li>
 	<li>If I discover I'm out of milk for my cereal, I'll sometimes eat a few bites of dry cereal before I put the box away!</li>
</ol>
