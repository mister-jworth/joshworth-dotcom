<p>The Teylers Museum in the Netherlands is presenting <em>Out of This World - The Search for Planets</em>. It's a fascinating exhibit that deals with the history of space exploration.</p>
<p>Besides the beautiful exhibit design (that makes exquisite use of vintage sci-fi art), one highlight for visitors is, of course, the chance to sit down at a computer and try out "<a title="A Tediously Accurate Map of the Solar System" href="/posts/a-tediously-accurate-map-of-the-solar-system">If the Moon Were Only One Pixel</a>."</p>
<p>If you happen to be somewhere near Haarlem, be sure to stop by and contemplate your place in the universe. <a href="http://www.teylersmuseum.eu/index.php?item=71&amp;flact=1&amp;lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Click here for more info about the exhibit. </a></p>
