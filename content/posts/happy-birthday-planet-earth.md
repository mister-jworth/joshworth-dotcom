<p>Today is Earth Day - which, I think is a celebration of the day the Earth was born. No one is really sure when this occurred, but most people think it was some time in April.</p>
<p>It makes sense that this day has been added to the calendar, since other celestial bodies get a day in their honor every week. (Monday = Moon Day, Saturday = Saturn Day, Sunday = Sun Day )</p>
<p>I would like to see more days that honor the things we take for granted. Air Day, Light Day, Gravity Day, Day Day, Night Day...</p>
