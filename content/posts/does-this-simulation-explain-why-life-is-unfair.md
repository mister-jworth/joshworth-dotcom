<p>If you had a room full of 100 people and gave each of them a dollar, then every day, had each person give a dollar to another randomly-selected person, what do you think would happen?</p>
<p>I first read about this simulation in a <a href="http://www.decisionsciencenews.com/?p=6221" target="_blank" rel="noopener">blog post</a> several years ago, and the results really surprised me. I programmed my own version using p5.js while working on a video for <a href="http://www.comingle.us" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Comingle</a> exploring the nature of wealth inequality.</p>
