<p>Longevity experts say death will eventually be a thing of the past. Once we're able to manipulate our bodies with cellular precision or digitize our brain patterns so they can be backed-up and transferred to a fresh new body, we'll be able to live on and on forever and ever. That'll be great for me, since I've got a ton of projects that I just don't have time for right now. I've started a to-do list so I don't forget.
<h2>1</h2>
Stop and smell the roses.<br />
All of them.<br />
Rate their scents on a scale of 1-1000.
<h2>2</h2>
Catch up on all those past issues of <em>National Geographic</em>.
<h2>3</h2>
Proofread the internet.
<h2>4</h2>
Binge watch <em>Days of our Lives</em>
<h2>5</h2>
Get a new cat.<br />
Film every moment of its life. When the cat dies, edit down the footage to one year's-worth of highlights to create the greatest cat video ever.<br />
Do a year-long screening of the film whenever I miss the cat.
<h2>6</h2>
Eat at every McDonalds.
<h2>7</h2>
Mess around with evolution.<br />
Do some selective breeding to create:
<ul><br />
 	<li>Domesticated squirrels.</li>
 	<li>Bi-pedal wiener dogs.</li>
 	<li>Praying mantises to match my wallpaper.</li>
 	<li>A species of bird with a call that sounds like <em>I'm Like a Bird</em> by Nelly Furtado</li>
</ul>
<h2>8</h2>
Figure out once and for all how many blades of grass are on the front lawn by counting them individually.
<h2>9</h2>
Finally finish that Ken Burns <em>Baseball</em> documentary. Really take the time to study it. When it mentions a particular game, pause the documentary, track down a recording of that game and watch the whole thing.<br />
When it mentions a particular player, do the same thing, but with every game in which that player appeared.
<h2>10</h2>
Open a museum for my socks.
<h2>11</h2>
Stage that one-man show I've always wanted to do: <em>The Life and Times of Herbert Hoover</em>. Reenact his whole life from birth to death. In real-time.<br />
If it's a hit, do all the other presidents.
<h2>12</h2>
Adopt a pet rock. Name it Roxy.<br />
Leave it outside.<br />
When it erodes down to almost nothing, change it’s name to Dusty.
<h2>13</h2>
Get into competitive tree racing.
<h2>14</h2>
Do one sit-up a year for 100 millennia. That's 100,000 reps. I'll have abs of steel!
<h2>15</h2>
Open a beach resort in Pittsburgh.
<h2>16</h2>
Start stockpiling sunscreen for when the Earth gets closer to the sun.
<h2>17</h2>
Write a series of books about a family. It will be a generation-spanning family saga. In each book, a different family member is the main character and you see the whole story from their perspective. Oh - and also the family is a family of bees.
<h2>18</h2>
Start planning my "End of the Holocene" party.<br />
It's going to be epoch.</p>
