<h3><em>Best questions to discuss at brunch while slowly stirring a Bloody Mary with a half-chewed piece of celery:</em></h3>
<ol>
	<li>Who should accompany your daughter to the Spring Cotillion now that Blake is out of the picture?</li>
	<li>Is Pocket-Full-O-Miracles ready for the stakes?</li>
	<li>Would Annabelle be upset if you included 'The Cape Cod Incident?" in your memoirs?</li>
	<li>Who will be using the summer house on the 4th of July?</li>
	<li>Should you make the big announcement next weekend, or wait for Granddad's retirement party?</li>
	<li>What can be done if Charles fails to repay his loan from the family trust?</li>
	<li>If they don't make you a partner after this case, should you start looking at other firms?</li>
	<li>Should you renew the club membership now that they've removed the clay courts?</li>
	<li>If Glen was cheating, does that nullify the prenup?</li>
	<li>When did they start adding so much Worcestershire to this mix?</li>
</ol>
