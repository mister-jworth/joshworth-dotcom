<p>Several years ago, while browsing at a thrift store, Maureen and I came across a series of old books called <em><a href="https://www.etsy.com/listing/246179745/vintage-24-volume-set-of-out-of-this?ref=market" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Out of This World - The Illustrated Library of the Bizarre and Extraordinary</a></em>. One of the stories contained within the collection was the amazing account of a group of Germans who decided to abandon civilization and move to an island in the Galapagos in order to create a utopian society. Intrigued by the cast of eccentric characters and the mysterious events that transpired on the island, I decided to do some of my own research and soon discovered a goldmine of letters, photos and film at the USC library.<br />
Over the course of several years, I took the raw material of this incredible story and turned it into a slightly fictionalized account in the form of a play which I finally completed during a stay at the <a href="http://www.yaddo.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Yaddo</a> artists' retreat in Saratoga Springs New York.</p>
