Asteroid Close Calls
Under the right circumstances, asteroids just 20 meters wide can destroy a city. Asteroids with possible diameters this size have passed or will pass closer to Earth than the Moon 262 times. This chart shows each flyby at its relative distance between Earth and the Moon. The asteroids are not to scale; in reality they would be too small to see.
